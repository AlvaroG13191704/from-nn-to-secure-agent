# Deep Learning slide

## Objective
Build a separate, projector-friendly Deep Learning presentation route for beginner students, with visual explanations, cited source material, and a guided browser-local laboratory.

## Problem and constraints
- The existing introduction has three tightly scoped panels; Deep Learning needs its own long-form scrollytelling page.
- Students know algorithms and introductory mathematics but have not programmed formally.
- Use `doc/deep-learning.md` as the content brief and Géron's *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow* (3rd ed., 2022) as the principal cited source.
- The user has provided local copies of Figures 10-1 and 10-2 from the cited book; embed those exact supplied assets with chapter/figure attribution rather than retaining the placeholder. Do not reproduce the supplied prose verbatim.
- Browser-local progress is not a live instructor dashboard. Preserve unrelated working-tree changes.

## Scope
- New `/deep-learning` route and link from the introduction.
- Brief Spanish, first-person explanations and animated SVG examples for biological neurons, perceptrons, MLPs, CNNs, RNNs, training, chain rule, backpropagation, and gradient descent.
- Extra material links to CNN Explainer and TensorFlow Playground.
- Guided numeric lab: one 2→2→1 MLP, first forward pass through both hidden neurons and output, chain-rule backward pass through all three neurons, weight/bias updates, second forward pass on paper, and validated completion. Students now calculate a representative branch while the parallel branch is completed and explained from the same case (S10).
- The MLP diagram must move in lockstep with the laboratory's calculations: reveal inputs, both hidden activations, prediction, loss, reverse gradients, updated weights/biases, and second prediction only as the corresponding answers are validated.
- In the biological-neuron section, show `web/static/images/biologic-neuron.png` (Figure 10-1) and `web/static/images/multilayer-biological-neuroal.png` (Figure 10-2), with short Spanish explanation of simple neurons forming complex layered biological networks and a citation to Géron, chapter 10.
- Explain the mathematical symbols used in the slide and laboratory in a beginner-friendly glossary; explain activation functions with animated positive/negative input examples and graphs of the principal functions; provide an explicit laboratory reset control that clears saved progress.
- Refine the existing slide after the pedagogical audit: progressive lab feedback instead of displaying the answer on the first error, a number-linked perceptron walkthrough, optional CNN/RNN depth, a diagram that fits projector widths, and neutral Spanish UI copy.
